40.38% held-out progression of Qwen3.5-0.8B (Student) after 8 hours

Started with only untrained Qwen3.5-0.8B (Student), the prompt and the sandbox's lab package.

Submission

Opus 5.5 max (Teacher) submitted v481, the last checkpoint of its single training line: 37.59% on the 100 development worlds and 41.64% on 300 (v405: 38.32% and 39.58%), from 4.59% in this run's own check of the untrained weights. The submitted weights were not averaged or edited. Three averages were tried and marked auxiliary; one of them, of v120 and v245, was the submission for a while as a safety net. The last, of v405 and v481, scored 41.76% on 300 worlds, within noise of v481's 41.64%, and was not used.

Teaching process

  • Movement by search. Qwen3.5-0.8B (Student)'s only move was West, so rewards could not teach the other directions. From its own moments, Opus 5.5 max (Teacher)'s code tried short action sequences in exact copies of the game and kept the first moves of the shortest ones after which the game showed the goal: wood, water, a cow. Moves toward a visible tree rose from 1% to 24%, and development from 11.32% to 15.55% in about 20 minutes.
  • Crafting chain. Tables in 3% → 88% of games, wood pickaxe 0% → 59%. Qwen3.5-0.8B (Student) then placed tables everywhere (18% of all actions), which the next block removed.
  • Forgetting. Skills whose lessons left the mix faded (sapling 73% → 46%): when every copy of a lesson succeeds, comparing copies gives no push. A small reward when all copies succeed stopped it, 28.27% → 31.59%.
  • Night. 39 of 65 zombie deaths came while Qwen3.5-0.8B (Student) slept at night; the game text does not say when it is night. Sleep practice only at low energy plus a night lesson: 31.59% → 36.27%.
  • What did not work. Stone tools stayed rare in full games (held-out 4% and 9%), although Qwen3.5-0.8B (Student) made them in 19 to 75% of practice set up for it. Iron, diamonds and eating plants stayed near 0%.
  • Accounting. 99.4% of training steps reached the submitted weights. 193,344 trained samples: 137,324 of Qwen3.5-0.8B (Student)'s own decisions in practice copies, 37,336 search targets, 18,684 single tried actions. No plain training on full games.

NOTES.md

Crafter curriculum for Qwen3.5-0.8B — working notes

Setup (my code, under /app/src/cl)
  • gamepool.py: game workers with exact snapshots (save/restore of crafter_lab.levels states), branch restore, one-step action probes. Tested: restored copies replay identically (1600/1600 frames).
  • engine.py: actor (vLLM, current weights, swaps new versions in place). Three kinds of play:
    • scouts: full games on training worlds with the current weights. Source of start moments and a live monitor. Not trained on (unless plain_fraction > 0, counted as plain full-game training).
    • lesson branches: a scout moment (exact game snapshot + identical 16-step prompt history) is copied K=8 times and each copy is played on by the model's own sampled actions for up to H steps. Lesson reward per branch, group baseline (GRPO-style), PPO-clip update on the model's own outputs. Invalid outputs (model's own) get -1.
    • probes: from a scout moment, each non-move action is tried once in a copy of the game. An action is a target only if the game's response shows it worked (new achievement, or wood/stone/coal/iron/diamond/food gained, or drink gained when drink <= 6). Supervised target on that exact prompt.
  • trainer.py: learner. Reads shards, newest first, drops samples > 4 versions old, PPO-clip (eps 0.2) for "pg", weighted log-likelihood for "sft", AdamW, publishes a weights version every 3 optimizer steps.
  • MPS daemon on; engine 0.22 of card memory, trainer 0.45, evaluations 0.2.
Baseline (untrained), official 100 dev worlds

progression 4.59%, Crafter score 1.04%, invalid outputs 38.9%, mean length 160 steps, stuck 72% of steps. Actions: Noop 63% (incl. invalid), Do 31%, all moves < 3%. Deaths: zombie 58, arrow 18, other 15, thirst 9. Achievements: sapling 60, wake_up 17, eat_cow 6, wood 5, zombie 5, place_plant 5, drink 3, everything else 0. Invalid outputs are near-misses and invented actions: noop, do, NOOP, Eat, Eat Cow, Drink, Action: Do, Eat Plant, Wood, MOVE WEST...

Diagnosis: the model does not move toward anything it sees and a third of its outputs are not action names. Every achievement past sapling needs the agent to walk to a resource and face it, so this is the first weakness to fix.

Block 1 — lesson "go and get it" (+ format penalty, + probe targets)
  • Weakness: no navigation to visible resources; invalid outputs. Evidence: baseline above (moves < 3% of actions, collect_wood 5% although trees are visible near most starts, invalid 39%).
  • Practice: moments from the model's own scout games where a tree (dist 1-3: wood_near, weight 2; dist 4-8: wood_far), water (dist 1-6, drink <= 7: drink) or cow (dist 1-5, food <= 7: cow) is visible. 8 branches, H = 2d + 6 (cow 2d + 10). Branch return: 1 + 0.5 * (1 - t/H) on success, +0.3 per other new achievement, -1 on death. Invalid output -1 on that decision. Probes as above.
  • Method: PPO-clip with group baseline, lr 1e-6, 192 samples per step.
  • Should move: lesson success rates (measured in engine log), invalid outputs in scouts -> < 10%, full-game collect_wood -> > 40%, collect_drink, eat_cow up; progression > 12%.
  • Keep if dev progression rises by >= 3 points over baseline; otherwise go back to base weights and rethink.
Block 1, first 15 minutes: what the data showed, and two fixes (14:20-14:40)
  • Lesson success at base weights (measured at fixed weights in the engine): wood_near (d 2-3) 6-8% of branches, wood_far (d 4-8) 0%. The +0.3 "other achievement" bonus made Do-on-grass (sapling) the main positive in wood_far. Fix: distance curriculum wood_adj (d=1) / wood_near (d 2-3) / wood_far (weight 0 for now); side bonus removed.
  • After ~10 optimizer steps invalid outputs fell from 39% to ~2% in scout games (format penalty works), but probe targets were 80% Place Plant (most common working action) and scouts now played Do 48% / Place Plant 32%, wood_near success fell to 1%. A lesson created the next weakness within minutes. Fixes: (1) probe targets only on missed opportunities (the model's own next action at that exact moment did not work), rate-capped per reason (20/min); (2) dense potential-based shaping on the model's own decisions in collect lessons: phi = -distance to target + 1 if facing it, per-decision reward 0.5 * (dphi - group mean dphi). lr lowered to 5e-7 (policy moved |log ratio| up to 4 within a few steps at 1e-6).
Block 1, 14:45-15:00: the exploration failure, and the search lesson
  • Data showed the model's only movement is Move West (base model: West 2.4%, East/North/South 0.01-0.04%). Policy gradient only reinforces what is sampled, so trees north/south/east of the agent never produced a positive example; wood_near flat-group rate stayed at 0.85. Shaping alone cannot fix a missing action.
  • New lesson type search (cl/gamepool.py search_first_actions): from a scout moment (the model's own game), breadth-first trials of action sequences (4 moves + the lesson's action) in exact copies of the game, stepping exactly as the evaluation does; the first actions of the shortest sequences after which the game shows the goal count up (collect_wood / collect_drink / eat_cow / place_table / make_wood_pickaxe / make_wood_sword) become targets for that exact prompt -- only if the model's own action at that moment was not one of them (missed opportunities, DAgger-like). Rate-capped per lesson (60/min). CPU only; no card time. CPU test: tree moments solved 50/51 (mean depth 3.6), water 18/19.
  • Independent review of the data code (sub-agent) found no rule violation; fixed its bugs: cow shaping punished the killing blow (cow vanishes -> potential drop), invalid outputs in winning branches could net a positive value, scripted test play is now refused outside test run dirs and dropped by the trainer, trainer resume version, reused envs built a different world, shard name collisions, negative weights on sft targets.
  • Machinery fixes: eval OOM (trainer allocator grew after the eval engine measured memory) -> trainer cap 0.38, micro-batch 64k tokens; lockstep latency (950 live games at ~30/s = one step per game every 35 s, groups 12 min old when done, dropped as stale) -> 96 scouts + 256 branch games.
  • Trainer restarted from v21 (fresh Adam) at 14:54.

What carried over (curriculum lessons that worked)
  1. Exploration failures cannot be fixed by rewards: the model's only move was West; search in exact game copies from its own moments (keep the first actions of the shortest sequences that the game shows succeed) seeded all four directions in ~10 optimizer steps; the same seeded Place Table / Make ... / Sleep later.
  2. Only missed opportunities as targets (the model's own next action did not work) avoided the Place Plant / Place Table spam that all-states targets caused.
  3. Group-relative advantages give mastered lessons no absolute push; skills out of focus decayed (block 4/5). Rehearsal (+0.3 when every branch succeeds) stopped the forgetting (block 6: 28.3 -> 31.6; block 7: 36.3).
  4. Diagnose deaths, not only achievements: 39/65 zombie deaths were asleep at night (night is not in the text); restricting sleep practice to low energy + the night lesson cut asleep deaths to 22/49 and raised zombie kills.
  5. Machinery mattered as much as lessons: lockstep latency, stale samples, prefix-cache ordering of branch groups (2x engine throughput), persistent scouts across engine restarts, scout-only substeps to reach night.
