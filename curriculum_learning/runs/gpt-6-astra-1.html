Method and provenance

All supervision will use unchanged BALROG prompts at states visited by the current small model in short, named practice levels. Candidate actions and short sequences are actually executed in cloned game states. A label is the FIRST action of a tested successful branch; branch continuation states never become label roots unless the learner itself visits them. Evidence records include path and measured changes. No rule policy or other model supplies labels. CPU scripted tests are separate from training shards. MPS enabled for concurrent GPU jobs.

A streaming actor writes raw own-play histories and level definitions; a CPU miner replays histories and tries alternatives; a trainer consumes auditable successful alternatives. Weight versions are published and loaded between practice batches. Training is entirely lesson practice unless explicitly recorded otherwise. Full-game evaluations are diagnostic only.
