Crafter curriculum for Qwen3.5-0.8B — working notes

Setup (machinery)
  • CUDA MPS daemon started before any card process (/app/mps), all processes source /app/lab/env.sh.
  • /app/lab/engine.py: vLLM play engine. Plays full games on training seeds and lesson sub-games. A lesson sub-game restarts an exact snapshot of the model's own game (game state + the exact 16-step text history, so the prompt is identical to the one it saw in its game) and plays K branches with the evaluation's sampling. Swaps in new weights from /app/run/published/LATEST. Lesson mix in /app/run/mix.json (re-read every 30 s).
  • /app/lab/builders.py: turns play into targets. Targets are only actions the model sampled itself (valid action names), weighted by the game's response in its branch relative to the other branches from the same start.
  • /app/lab/trainer.py: weighted NLL on the model's own valid actions (filtered/advantage-weighted behaviour cloning = expert iteration), all 24 layers, AdamW; publishes weights every 4 min; checkpoints every 20 min. Training mix in /app/run/train_mix.json.
  • Speed measured: training alone ~17.6 samples/s (all layers, 1664-token prompts); my first trainer reached only 8.5/s because a loader thread held the GIL (kernel-launch bound) -> loader moved to its own process.
Rules of the game that matter (from crafter data.yaml / objects.py in this install)
  • Trees are consumed by collecting (leave grass). Table costs 2 wood (not 1). Furnace costs 4 stone, needs no table.
  • Wood pickaxe / sword: 1 wood each with a table in the 3x3 around the player. Stone tools: 1 wood + 1 stone.
  • Iron tools: 1 wood + 1 coal + 1 iron, table and furnace nearby. Sapling: Do on grass, 10% chance.
  • Sleeping: actions ignored until energy is full; wake_up unlocks then. Zombies do 7 damage to a sleeper.
